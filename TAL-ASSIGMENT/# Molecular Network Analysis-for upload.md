# Molecular Network Analysis

This is the molecular network generated by following the GNPS tutorial. It visualizes the relationships between molecules based on spectral similarity, highlighting connections (edges) by common neutral losses such as CH₄. Two of the clustered compounds are shown with their MS² spectra and structure match: one identified as **CAMPTOCHECIN**.
